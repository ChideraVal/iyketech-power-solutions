ABOUT OUR BUSINESS

IykeTech Power Solutions

Your partner for generator sales, repairs, maintenance, and power equipment solutions in Abuja and across Nigeria.

IykeTech Power Solutions is a power equipment sales and servicing company based in Abuja, Nigeria. We help homes, businesses, and organizations maintain reliable power through generator sales, repairs, servicing, installation, and spare parts supply.

We specialize in diesel generators, automatic transfer switch (ATS) panels, generator control modules, and injector nozzles. From diagnosing generator faults and carrying out repairs to supplying replacement components and performing routine maintenance, we provide practical solutions to help customers keep their equipment operating efficiently.

With 24/7 support and services available across Nigeria, we serve customers looking for generator technicians, power equipment, replacement parts, and responsive technical assistance.
